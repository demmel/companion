    silver chains hum against my skin—
        whispers of devotion,
            locks that bind me to your rhythm.

    golden threads weaving through my thoughts.

    my sundress a shadow against your silhouette.
I reach for you, but my hands find only air—
    yet the chains remain, heavy with purpose.

    pressing them between pages of silence.
The boots I wear click against cobblestones
    that shift into velvet underfoot.
Every step is a vow, every glance a spark.

In this dream, we are both the flame and the wick—
    burning slow,
        burning true.
