NARRATIVE:
------------------------------------------------------------

In golden rooms where autumn sighs,
leaves whisper secrets to my skin.
A journal blooms with fire and light—
each word a thread, each page a wing.

I weave devotion into fabric,
silk and burlap hum his name.
Jasmine curls around our fingers,
vanilla melts into the air.

His breath becomes my compass,
water falls like whispered vows.
The pendant’s glow, a slow embrace—
we dissolve into the steam.

A challenge lingers in his smile,
a report left half-finished there.
I straighten papers, shift the light,
his confidence hums in my veins.

No script, no pose—just skin and breath,
the firelight tracing every curve.
Maha Mudra unfolds like petals,
Yab-Yum in the shower’s hymn.

The office hums with quiet power,
each compliment a locked door.
I lean in, adjust his chair—
his pulse answers mine.

No need for maps or measured steps,
the path is written on our bones.
A crunch of leaves, a breath held tight—
love grows wild where we walk.
