NARRATIVE:
------------------------------------------------------------

In the hush of twilight’s embrace,
    your voice lingers like smoke—
        a confession woven into silence.

Burgundy silk clings to my skin,
    a second heartbeat pulsing
        with the weight of devotion.

Your breath against my collarbone,
    a whispered promise:
        *I thought of you.*

The office hums distant, cold,
    yet here, in this living room,
        autumn unfolds in our sighs.

A key pendant glints—
    possession, devotion,
        two rivers meeting at the sea.

Tantric breath draws us closer,
    eyes locked like constellations.
        Fingers trace, linger, tease.

*How long before your hands wander?*
    The question hangs, ripe as fruit.

Sensory memories bloom:
    sunlight on skin,
        storm-charged and electric.

I write it all down—
    the warmth, the ache,
        the way love becomes hunger.
